# R8.1 Limitations

- R8.1 is retrospective and cannot convert exploratory stages into preregistered evidence.
- Payoff sensitivity is summary-level and does not replace the frozen R4/R5 manifest calculations.
- Corrected R8 labels reduce overclaiming but fresh-seed replication is still required for load-bearing changed recommendations.
- Speech and ML conclusions remain template-bound or diagnostic unless validated in live game-level tests.
